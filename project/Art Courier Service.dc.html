Art Courier Service

Art Courier Transportation, Nevada to California

Fast, secure moving routes between Las Vegas, Los Angeles, and San Francisco — expertly handling fine art, statues, sculptures, and collections of any size.

Overview

Save Time and Money With Our Art Courier Service

Our commercially licensed, professionally trained drivers move fine art securely between Las Vegas, Los Angeles, and San Francisco — with warehouse storage and drop-off available in each city. As an industry leader for over 25 years, we built our business on packing and shipping art of every shape and size, and expanded our fleet as our warehousing grew.

Our Team

Professional Art Movers in Las Vegas

Licensed. Qualified. Certified. Reliable.

Specialized Handling

Trained professionals who understand the unique requirements of art packing, handling, and transportation, protecting against damage from improper handling, environmental conditions, or transit shocks.

Custom Packaging

From soft packing for less delicate items to custom-built wood crates for fragile or high-value pieces, especially for air travel where rigid protection and stable humidity/temperature control matter most.

Insurance & Security

Full insurance coverage plus GPS tracking, in-vehicle security, and secure, climate-controlled storage during stops or layovers to protect against theft or environmental damage.

Before, during, and after transport, we document every piece's condition in detail — including photos and video condition reports — to protect you in the rare event of an insurance claim or dispute.

Logistics

Climate Controlled Art Moving and Logistics

Climate Control

Transport vehicles equipped to maintain museum- and gallery-like conditions throughout the journey, critical for long-distance and international shipments.

Logistics Coordination

Careful planning around mode of transport, size, weight, and fragility, customs handling for international shipments, and dedicated couriers who may accompany especially valuable pieces.

Our Process

How We Handle Fine Art Moving

Moving art from Las Vegas to California takes detailed logistics planning. We leverage our network and deep understanding of local and interstate routes to minimize risk and transit time. Our white-glove service includes pickup directly from your location, secure loading onto climate-controlled vehicles, and careful delivery to the destination — with insurance options to cover any unforeseen damage. Whether a piece is headed to a gallery in Los Angeles, a museum in San Francisco, or a private collection, it arrives in the same condition it left in.

Route

The Route

LAS VEGAS TO LOS ANGELES

Our routes start at our Las Vegas headquarters, with on-site short and long-term storage until your piece is ready for delivery to LA. Pick up or drop off anytime during business hours.

LOS ANGELES TO SAN FRANCISCO

Flexible scheduling to meet your deadlines, with proper warehouse facilities in San Francisco to store and deliver any request.

SAN FRANCISCO TO LAS VEGAS

A direct route back to Nevada, with on-site boxing and crating, large-format art removal, and full installation service at your final destination.
