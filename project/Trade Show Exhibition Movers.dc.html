Spotlight

Two of Las Vegas's Biggest Shows

SEMA (Specialty Equipment Market Association)

Held annually at the Las Vegas Convention Center (2025 dates: November 4–7). Over 160,000 attendees from 140+ countries, 2,200+ exhibiting companies, and nearly 4.6 million square feet of automotive innovation — from custom cars to performance parts, plus live drifting demos and its renowned New Products Showcase.

CES (Consumer Electronics Show)

Held annually in early January, open to both industry professionals and the public. Over 4,500 exhibiting companies and around 170,000 attendees, spotlighting consumer electronics, AI, automotive tech, and health tech through keynotes and major product launches.

The Venue

Las Vegas Convention Center

One of the largest and busiest convention facilities in the world, the LVCC spans 4.6 million square feet — 2.5 million dedicated to exhibits alone — with 225 meeting rooms and two grand lobbies. It's connected by the Las Vegas Monorail and the Tesla-powered Vegas Loop, cutting travel time across the massive campus. The 2021 West Hall expansion added 1.4 million square feet, and a $600 million renovation is modernizing the North, Central, and South halls with new digital signage and technology.

Our Process

Moving Logistics for Special Events

We handle pre-show storage, transportation, and on-site handling through to post-show shipping — including advance warehousing with full tracking and security. On-site, our teams manage move-in and move-out, local union labor regulations, and booth assembly and disassembly, using air-ride suspension trucks and color-coded labeling to prevent damage and keep everything moving efficiently.
