About Us

Providing Customized and Affordable Solutions

Las Vegas Crating & Warehouse Logistics is a local trusted resource and industry leader in custom crates, competitive carrier rates, large-format packing, and on-time shipping — with the same motto since day one: we ship anything to anywhere.

Our Story

From Humble Boxes and Beginnings

Founded over two decades ago as Box Brothers, a packing and shipping shop in Santa Monica that opened in 1985, our roots are humble: hand-made boxes, a storefront, and a commitment to getting things there safely.

Growth led us to open Las Vegas Crating & Logistics in Las Vegas, Nevada, expanding from hand-made boxes into custom wood crates — a shift that required warehouse space, trucks, and a growing team to keep up with demand.

We outgrew our first location and moved into the industrial complex warehouse we continue to expand today. In 2023, we launched full-service short and long-term warehouse storage to house everything from cars and arcade machines to vintage signage and fine art.

Starting at a modest 10,000 square feet, our warehouse has since more than quadrupled in size — while keeping the same core team, now shipping and receiving worldwide.
