Did You Know

Fun Facts About Las Vegas Trade Shows

1
Global Hub

Known as the "Trade Show Capital of the World," hosting more than 22,000 conventions and trade shows annually.

2
Massive Exhibitions

CES alone occupies nearly 3 million square feet of exhibit space with over 4,500 exhibitors.

3
Economic Impact

Trade shows generate billions for the local economy, with more than $11 billion coming from business travelers each year.

4
Industry Variety

Covering everything from tech (CES) and fashion (MAGIC) to construction (World of Concrete) and broadcasting (NAB Show).

5
Unique Events

Niche shows like the Shoe Show (WSA) and the Global Gaming Expo (G2E) bring specialized markets to the city.

6
Convenient Access

Over 150,000 hotel rooms and 15 million square feet of exhibition space make it easy to host massive crowds.
