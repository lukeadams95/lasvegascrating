Lithium Battery Shipping & Storage

Lithium batteries are classified as a dangerous good under DOT Class 9, with strict safety requirements — we manage every part of packing, shipping, transporting, and storing lithium-related cargo in full compliance.

Battery Types

Lithium-ion (rechargeable, found in laptops, tablets, phones, and hybrid vehicles) and lithium metal (non-rechargeable, used in hearing aids, watches, smoke detectors, and cameras) — each regulated under one of 8 UN classification codes depending on how it’s packaged and shipped.

Process

How It's Applied

Classify the Shipment

We determine the correct UN classification based on battery chemistry and packaging type.

Package to Spec

Batteries are packed to meet Hazardous Materials Regulations for marking, labeling, and cushioning.

Document & Ship

Full shipping papers and certification are prepared before your cargo ever leaves our dock.
