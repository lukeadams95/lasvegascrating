Corrugated Shipping Boxes

Corrugated shipping boxes are sturdy, lightweight containers made from corrugated fiberboard — a wavy inner “fluting” sandwiched between two flat “linerboards” that creates cushioning, rigidity, and impact resistance. They’re the backbone of modern logistics, eco-friendly and fully recyclable.

Materials & Construction

Kraft paper linerboards with a fluted medium for support. Single-Wall for lighter items, Double-Wall for heavier or fragile loads, Triple-Wall for industrial-strength jobs. Flute sizes range from A-flute (maximum cushioning) to B-flute (better stacking), sourced to meet burst strength and edge crush standards.

Process

How It's Applied

Select the Right Box

We help you choose the correct wall strength, flute type, and size for your item.

Protect & Cushion

Foam inserts, bubble wrap, or dividers are added based on what's inside.

Seal & Label

Boxes are sealed, labeled, and prepped for pickup, shipping, or storage.

Sizes & Types
RSC — 4×4×4″ HSC — 12×12×12″ FOL — 18×18×18″ OSC — 24×24×24″ Telescoping Die-Cut / Custom

Where Are Corrugated Boxes Used?

Their shock-absorbing design protects goods during transit and can be combined with wood reinforcement for oversized or international shipments.

Durable enough for long or short-term storage, with moisture-barrier options suited to Las Vegas's dry climate.

Anti-static liners and double-wall construction protect TVs, computers, and other sensitive equipment.

Acid-free tissue, foam corners, and telescoping designs adjust to fit frames, canvases, or sculptures.

Molded pulp dividers and custom inserts protect bottles and fragile heirlooms alike.

Heat Shrink Wrapping

A flexible, heat-sensitive plastic film is wrapped around an item and heated to shrink tightly around its shape, creating a secure, tamper-evident seal that protects against moisture, dust, and UV.

Materials

Polyethylene, PVC, or polyolefin film, shrinking at 250°F–350°F, in thicknesses from 6–12 mils for heavier items, with UV-inhibiting, flame-retardant, or anti-static additives available.

Process

How It's Applied

Wrap the Item

Film is draped or wrapped around the cargo with full coverage.

Apply Controlled Heat

Heat guns, blowers, or shrink tunnels shrink the film tightly into place.

Inspect the Seal

Every wrap is checked for gaps to guarantee a weatherproof finish.

Where Is Heat Shrink Wrapping Used?

Yes, for general cargo, palletized goods, and retail products in transit.

Industrial Shrink Wrapping

Protects oversized or irregularly shaped cargo across marine, construction, aerospace, energy, and defense industries — the film conforms to complex shapes for a tight, weatherproof barrier.

Vapor Barrier Bags

Also called foil, alufoil, or Mylar bags — one of the most effective packaging solutions against moisture, oxygen, salt spray, and airborne contaminants.

Process

How It's Applied

Seal the Bag
