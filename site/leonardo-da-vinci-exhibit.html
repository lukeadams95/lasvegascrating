Case Study

Las Vegas Crating Packs and Ships the Leonardo Da Vinci Exhibit

A once-in-a-lifetime job — safely packing and shipping a world-renowned traveling exhibition of da Vinci’s inventions, art, and scientific discoveries.

The Exhibit

Time to Pack Up and Ship the Da Vinci Exhibition

Developed by the Anthropos Association of Italy and Grande Exhibitions, under the Consulate of the Italian Cultural Institute, “The Da Vinci Exhibition” brought over 11 themed areas of da Vinci’s genius to The Venetian on the Las Vegas Strip — featuring replicas of his most famous inventions like the flying machine, submarine, and helicopter, reproductions of masterworks including the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper, his anatomical and scientific studies, and immersive multimedia presentations. When it was time for the exhibit to move on, our team of white-glove specialists took on the job.

Speed & Security

The Fastest Way to Ship Valuable Art

Air freight is typically the preferred method for high-value, time-sensitive artwork — minimizing time in transit and reducing risk. Working with a specialized art transport team means proper crating, climate-controlled handling, and full coordination of permits and customs, balanced against the higher cost compared to ground or sea freight.

Cautionary Tales

The Stakes of Getting It Right

THE PEACOCK THRONE

Lost in 1739 when looted during an invasion of India; valued at an estimated $2 billion today.

LAOCOÖN AND HIS SONS

Lost at sea in 1798 during transport to France; valued at an estimated $45 million today.

THE CHOCOLATE VENUS

A life-size 1802 chocolate sculpture that melted in transit due to heat and humidity.

Rare, but a reminder of why proper handling and care during transport matters.
