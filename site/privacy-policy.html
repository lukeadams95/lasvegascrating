Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2024.

This Privacy Notice for Long's Crating and Logistics Inc. (doing business as Las Vegas Crating) describes how and why we access, collect, store, use, and/or share your personal information when you use our Services, including when you visit our website, use our packing, shipping, crating, and storage services, or otherwise engage with us. Questions or concerns? Contact us at contact@lasvegascrating.com.

Summary of Key Points
  • What personal information we process — depends on how you interact with us and the Services you use.
  • We do not process any sensitive personal information.
  • We do not collect any information from third parties.
  • How we process your information — to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, ensure security and prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • When and with whom we share personal information — only in specific situations, such as with vendors, service providers, or legal authorities.
  • How we keep your information safe — through appropriate technical and organizational security measures.
  • Your rights — depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including how to exercise them.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us. We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you request a quote, fill out a contact form, apply for a job, or otherwise communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any details you share about the items you need packed, crated, shipped, or stored.

We do not process any sensitive personal information (such as health data, biometric data, or racial or ethnic origin), and we do not collect any information about you from third parties.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, to communicate with you regarding quotes, bookings, and shipments, for security and fraud-prevention purposes, and to comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations, including customs and international shipping requirements.

3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On to Process Your Information?

We only process your personal information when we have a valid legal reason to do so, including with your consent, to fulfill a contract or service you have requested, to comply with legal obligations, to protect your vital interests, or for our legitimate business interests.

4. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We may share information with vendors and service providers who perform services on our behalf (such as freight carriers, customs brokers, and payment processors), during a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), or when required by law, court order, or governmental request.

5. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to gather usage and analytics information, remember your preferences, and improve the performance of our website. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements).

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, no electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized third parties will never be able to defeat our security measures.

8. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 12.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature that signals your preference not to be tracked online. Because there is not yet a uniform technology standard for recognizing DNT signals, we do not currently respond to them.
