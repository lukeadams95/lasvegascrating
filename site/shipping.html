International Shipping

25 years of shipping anything to anywhere, worldwide. What started as a local box company has grown into a multi-location international shipping hub, handling customs forms, tracking, and ISPM-15 heat-treated wood for every international shipment.

ISPM-15 Heat Treatment

Wood packaging is heat-treated to a core temperature of 132.8°F for a minimum of 30 minutes, eliminating wood-boring beetles, pinewood nematodes, wood-boring wasps, and fungal pathogens — required to clear international customs and avoid costly port delays.
