Re-Usable Plastic & Wood Crates

A durable, cost-effective, and sustainable alternative to single-use packaging, built for dozens or hundreds of trips — reducing waste and long-term costs compared to disposable boxes.

Plastic Crates

Made from HDPE or PP via injection molding, with reinforced corners, ergonomic handholds, and collapsible designs that fold flat without tools.

Wooden Crates

Built with a truss-like framework from softwoods or hardwoods, using demountable fasteners like lag screws and bolts so panels can be disassembled and reassembled multiple times without losing strength.

Reusable vs. Single-Use

Reusables withstand 50–700+ cycles versus one use, offer superior impact resistance and weatherproofing, and cut landfill waste significantly when reused enough times.

ISPM-15 Heat Treated Wood Shipping

Also known as the “wheat stamp,” ISPM-15 compliance is required for all wood packaging — crates, pallets, and dunnage — used in international shipping, ensuring wood is debarked, heat-treated, and properly stamped.

Treatment Types

Standard Heat Treatment (HT), Kiln-Dried (KD), Mobile HT, Portable Chamber Process, and Fast Container Connector — plus Methyl Bromide fumigation for container or tent treatment.

Process

How It's Applied

Heat to Core Temperature

Wood is heated until its core reaches 56°C (133°F) for a minimum of 30 minutes.

Certify & Stamp

Every treated batch is certified under American Lumber Standards Committee guidelines and stamped for compliance.

Inspect Before Transit

Professional inspectors verify certification before and after crossing international borders.

Treatment Types
Standard HT Kiln-Dried (KD) Mobile HT Portable Chamber Fast Container Connector Methyl Bromide Fumigation

What Requires ISPM-15 Treatment?
